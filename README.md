# travel-website

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏨 **Stay Facilities**  
  Find and book hotels, homestays, restaurants, and more—all curated to match your preferences.

- 🧭 **Customized Interests**  
  Select what you love (beaches, temples, adventure, wellness, etc.) and get a trip plan tailored to you.

- 💸 **Smart Trip Planning & Budgeting**  
  AI-generated itineraries, expense estimations, local transport predictions, and more.

- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Solo Traveler Connections**  
  Connect with fellow solo travelers and bike explorers heading your way.

- 🪷 **Wellness & Community Support**  
  Discover yoga retreats, meditation camps, and local support hubs for a serene travel experience.

- 🌟 **Local Events & Hidden Gems**  
  Explore festivals, cultural fairs, and offbeat destinations most tourists miss.

- 🍽️ **Prebooking for Restaurants & Food**  
  Pre-book seats and meals with a small advance to skip long queues.

- 💳 **Integrated Payment Gateway**  
  Secure and fast payments through **PayPal** and more (Coming Soon).

---

## 🏗️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React.js + Tailwind CSS + Shadcn UI  
- **Backend**: Django + Django REST Framework  
- **AI Integration**: Llama 3.2 for story & itinerary generation  
- **Database**: MySQL (Free Tier)  
- **3D UI**: Spline for immersive homepage experiences  
- **Authentication**: JWT-based user login system
